Here I am

FIG. 01

I'm a Computing student at Queen's University in Kingston, Ontario, Canada, on the Arts and Science Scholarship. I work mostly in Python, and what I care about is AI and machine learning, specifically the part where a model has to make sense of messy real-world signal rather than a clean dataset.

That problem and mindset started years ago with Archery. I shot competitively for years and coach now, which means a lot of evenings spent watching video and telling people what to change. So I worked on STRAIGHT, an AI and ML-based system that reads a practice clip, measures how consistently form is reproduced across shots, and returns coaching feedback in language a coach would use. It was accepted as a poster at ACM UIST.

I'm looking for software engineering internships and research positions, and I want to keep pushing further into ML: more depth, and more people to build it with.
